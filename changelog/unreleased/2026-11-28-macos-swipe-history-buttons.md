- **macOS: a horizontal swipe presses `PointerButton::Back` or `Forward`.** AppKit reports "back" and "forward" in two ways: buttons 3 and 4 of a mouse, which Rune already heard, and a swipe event (`swipeWithEvent:`), which a three-finger trackpad swipe sends and which some mice send for their side buttons. The vendored winit now turns a horizontal swipe into a press and release of `MouseButton::Back` (swipe left, positive `deltaX`) or `Forward`, so `El::pointer_buttons([Back, Forward])` and `El::on_button` hear both. A swipe reported in phases counts once, when it ends; a vertical swipe is ignored.
