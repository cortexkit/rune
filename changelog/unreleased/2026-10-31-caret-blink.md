- **The text caret blinks.** A focused field's caret shows for 0.5 s, then hides for 0.5 s, while its window is focused. Typing, moving the caret, changing the selection or focusing another field shows it again and restarts the cycle. The window wakes only when the caret turns on or off, two frames a second. An inactive window draws no caret and schedules no blink wake. When the window hasn't reported focus (as in headless snapshots), under reduced motion, or after 60 s without a change, a drawn caret stays on without waking the window. The caret asks for no frames between blink wakes.
