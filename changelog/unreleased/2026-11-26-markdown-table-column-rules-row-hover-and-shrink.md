- **`MarkdownStyle::table_column_rules`, `MarkdownStyle::table_row_hover` and `MarkdownStyle::table_shrink(TableShrink)`: lines between a Markdown table's columns, a hover overlay on its body rows, and a second rule for a table too wide for its text.** `table_column_rules(Some(c))` draws a hairline at the leading edge of every column after the first, the full height of the row, taking no room (the right edge in a right-to-left table). `table_row_hover(Some(c))` draws `c` over a body row while the pointer is over it, never over the header. `table_shrink(TableShrink::WidestFirst)` narrows the widest columns first: every column takes an equal share of the width and a column whose content needs less keeps its content's width, so a short column of labels or numbers stays on one line beside a column of prose; a full-width table still shares its spare room by content. All three are off by default (`None`, `None`, `TableShrink::Proportional`), so a table draws as before until a style asks for them. `TableShrink` is exported from `rune_kit`.
